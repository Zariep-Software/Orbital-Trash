
## 12. Visual and Audio Direction

### Visual style
A readable 2D arcade presentation is recommended for a one-week schedule.

- Earth and its atmosphere form the background.
- A clear circular ring marks the cleanup boundary.
- The rocket has a bright silhouette and visible thrust.
- Trash objects have distinct silhouettes.
- Pull and push magnets have different colors or particle patterns.
- Collisions use a brief flash and small particle burst; rocket destruction uses a compact explosion.

### Audio
- Rocket thrust loop
- Pull magnet activation
- Push pulse
- Trash crossing the cleanup boundary
- Collision / damage cue
- Rocket destruction
- Timer warning near the end of the round
